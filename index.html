05 — A LETTER, WITHOUT EXPECTATIONS

What I wish I could
explain gently.

A letter with no demand for an answer.

Hey,

I've been trying to find the right words for a while. I don't want this to sound like a complaint, and I really don't want you to feel guilty after reading it. This is simply me being honest about something I usually keep inside.

I care about you. A lot. Sometimes I get jealous, sometimes I feel possessive, and sometimes my thoughts become anxious over little things. I know those feelings are mine to work through. They aren't rules for you to follow, and they don't give me a right to limit your freedom or make you responsible for reassuring me all the time.

When replies are short or a conversation ends quickly, I sometimes don't know what to say next. I start wondering if I'm bothering you, if you're tired, or if something has changed. I know I can't know what's happening in your mind just by looking at a message. I wish I could stop turning every quiet moment into a question.

People sometimes tell me, “Maybe she didn't choose you,” or “Don't wait even one second for someone who can't choose you.” I hear them. I know they may be trying to protect me. But I can't pretend my feelings vanish just because someone says they should. Even if life doesn't turn out the way I hope, it doesn't make what I felt fake.

I understand that you don’t have feelings for anyone right now, and I respect that. I don’t want you to feel that you have to like me back or explain your feelings. What I feel is my own, and I never want it to become a responsibility for you. I simply wanted you to know how much you mean to me.

I want to treat you with kindness, remember the little things, listen to your stories and make you feel appreciated. I often put your comfort first because I never want my feelings to become pressure. But I'm also learning that I need to look after my own heart and express myself honestly instead of silently carrying everything.

When you're around and we talk naturally, it feels special to me. I don't need every minute of your day. I don't want forced conversations. I just value the moments we genuinely share, and sometimes I miss how effortless they felt.

If you need space, I will respect it. If your feelings are different from mine, I will respect that too. I don't want you to read this as a request to fix anything. I only wanted you to understand why I sometimes become quiet when, inside, I have so much to say.

Thank you for being part of my story, in whatever way feels right and comfortable for both of us.

With care,
someone who is learning to put feelings into words.
